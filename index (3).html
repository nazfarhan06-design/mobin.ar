Lining it up with the cabin on site

  1. Stand a few metres in front of the door (D1) side, with the cabin in view.
  2. Tap View on site in AR and slowly sweep the phone over the ground until it detects the floor.
  3. The model drops in at full size and can’t be scaled. Drag it to move it, and twist with two fingers to rotate, until its corners sit on the real cabin’s corners.
